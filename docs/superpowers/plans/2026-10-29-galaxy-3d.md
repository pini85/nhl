# Galaxy tab — a full-screen 3D view of the club

**Goal:** a new bottom-nav tab, **Galaxy** (`/galaxy`), that shows the club's data as a 3D scene with the best graphics a phone can run: real data, touch controls, bloom, and a smooth 60fps on mid-range phones.

## 1. Research notes

What the best WebGL/WebGPU work in 2025–2026 has in common (three.js forum Showcase, Codrops TSL/GPGPU tutorials, Three.js Journey "animated galaxy", Awwwards SOTD 3D sites, R3F performance guides):

- **Light builds on light.** Additive blending, no depth writes, soft round points with a smooth falloff, then a real **bloom** pass over a dark scene. Brightness above 1 (HDR) feeds the bloom.
- **Animation runs on the GPU.** Particles move in the vertex shader from a `uTime` uniform (for example inner stars orbit faster than outer ones, called differential rotation), so tens of thousands of points cost almost no JavaScript.
- **Small cinematic touches.** Film grain, a light vignette, slight chromatic aberration at the edges, a filmic tone map (AgX/ACES), star "diffraction spikes", a fresnel rim on spheres, an intro that expands from a single point.
- **Phones need a budget.** Cap pixel ratio at 2, start lower on weak devices, drop quality when FPS falls (drei `PerformanceMonitor`), no MSAA under bloom, instanced meshes, a fixed number of draw calls, mipmap bloom with fewer levels.

### Library choice

| Option | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| `three` 0.186 + `@react-three/fiber` 9.8 (React 19) | **Use.** Declarative scene in the same React app. R3F 9 supports React `>=19 <19.4`; the app is on 19.3. |
| `@react-three/drei` 10.7 | **Use** for `OrbitControls`, `PerformanceMonitor`, `MeshReflectorMaterial`, `Html`. |
| `@react-three/postprocessing` 3.2 + `postprocessing` 6.39 | **Use** for Bloom (mipmap blur), ChromaticAberration, Vignette, Noise, ToneMapping. One merged effect pass. |
| `maath` | **Use** for frame-rate-independent easing (`easing.damp3`) of camera flights and bars. |
| WebGPU renderer + TSL | **Not now.** Fast and supported on iOS 26 / Chrome, but three.js still marks it experimental and the pmndrs post-processing stack is WebGL only. WebGL2 with hand-written shaders gives the same look on every phone today. |

## 2. Design

Full-screen canvas behind a glass HUD. Three scenes with camera flights between them:

1. **Games — "Season galaxy".** Every saved game is a star on a spiral arm; oldest at the rim, newest next to a glowing core. Win = cyan-white, loss = crimson, OT loss = amber. Star size = goals in the game, height = goal difference. A glowing thread traces the timeline. Around it: tens of thousands of dust particles in spiral arms that orbit on the GPU, a far starfield, and the core glow. Tap a star → score, opponent, date, link to the game.
2. **Chemistry — "Constellation".** Each regular is a glowing planet (size = points, color = position: forwards blue, D green, goalies violet, you gold). Players who played together are linked by beams; thickness = games together, color = win % together (red → cyan). Energy pulses run along the beams. Layout from a small seeded 3D force simulation. Tap → name, GP, G-A-P, best partner.
3. **Skyline — "Leaders".** 3D bars on a reflective ice rink drawn in code (red/blue lines, faceoff circles), set in an arc. Stat chips: Points (goals + assists stacked), Goals, Assists, Hits, Shots. Bars spring to new heights when the stat changes. Tap → that player's numbers and a link to the player page.

Shared:

- Intro "big bang": everything grows out of the centre on load.
- Drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, slow auto-rotate that resumes 4 s after you let go. Tap vs drag is told apart by pointer travel; hit targets are larger than the visuals for thumbs. Short vibration on tap where supported.
- "You" (the `ddd-player` cookie) is highlighted in gold in every scene.
- Quality tiers: **high** (dpr up to 2, ~40k particles, reflections, full post) and **low** (dpr 1–1.25, ~14k particles, plain floor, bloom only). Start tier from device hints; `PerformanceMonitor` drops a tier when FPS falls and never bounces back up mid-session.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no intro, no auto-rotate, no orbiting dust.
- No WebGL: a friendly message, the rest of the app works.

## 3. Data

`lib/stats/galaxy.ts` (pure, unit-tested) builds one small serializable object on the server:

- `games`: id, time, GF, GA, outcome, OT, opponent — sorted oldest → newest.
- `players`: club skaters and goalies who are not guests (from `playerTotals`, the same numbers as Leaders), with main position.
- `links`: pairs of those players with ≥ 2 full games together, with wins together.
