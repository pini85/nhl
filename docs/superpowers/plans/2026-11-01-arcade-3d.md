# Arcade in 3D — notes and plan

The arcade game keeps its sim (`components/arcade/engine/`) exactly as it was: same physics, AI,
controls and tests. Only the view changed. A 3D arena now draws the match; the old canvas
renderer draws the HUD on top (scoreboard, banners, ticker, joystick, name tags, floating words).

## Library choice

- **three.js** (already in the app for the Galaxy tab, 0.186). Smallest download of the big three,
  biggest community, and the code we need is plain scene drawing, not a full engine.
- Not Babylon.js or PlayCanvas: both are bigger downloads and bring editors, physics and GUI we
  don't need. Our sim already handles collisions.
- Not React Three Fiber for the game itself: the game loop runs at a fixed 120 Hz step outside
  React, so plain three.js objects updated each frame are simpler and avoid re-renders.
- Not a physics engine (Rapier, cannon): arcade feel comes from hand-tuned rules, which the sim
  already has.

## Mechanics borrowed from classic arcade hockey

From NHL Hitz and NHL '94: 3-on-3, turbo bursts, big body checks, one-timers as the main way to
score, and "on fire" after a streak. Our sim already had all of these. The 3D view now shows them:

- **Skating stride**: legs push out to the side and back in turn, faster with speed; players
  crouch lower at speed and on a burst, and lean into turns.
- **Stick**: stickhandling sweep while carrying, slapshot wind-up following the charge, a
  follow-through after any shot, a stab forward on a poke check.
- **Hits**: the hitter drops a shoulder; the victim falls on the ice (and spins on a big hit),
  then gets up.
- **Goalies**: wide crouched stance with pads, blocker and catcher; drop into the butterfly on a save.
- **Goals**: scorer raises the stick, goal lamp spins, crowd jumps, net bulges, confetti.
- **Ice**: skate marks build up over the game; hard stops spray snow.

## Camera

Broadcast camera behind the home end, about 56° above the ice, following the puck up ice. "Up the
screen" is still "towards the goal we attack", so touch and keyboard controls didn't change. It
zooms in on goals (not with reduced motion) and shakes on hits.

## Files

- `components/arcade/render3d.ts`: the 3D view (camera, players, pucks, particles, markers), wraps the HUD.
- `components/arcade/three/player.ts`: the hockey player model and its animation.
- `components/arcade/three/arena.ts`: ice, boards, glass, nets, stands and crowd, lights.
- `components/arcade/render.ts`: gains a HUD-only mode and the `View` interface both views implement.
- Arcade menu: new "3D arena" toggle (on by default). Off = the classic top-down view. Without
  WebGL the game falls back to the classic view on its own.
